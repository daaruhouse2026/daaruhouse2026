Where Every Meal Tells a Story

DAARU HOUSE was born from a deep love for the royal cuisine of Hyderabad — that majestic city where Mughal grandeur met Deccan tradition. We bring those 400-year-old recipes, unchanged and uncompromised, to the streets of Thailand.

Every grain of our Dum Biryani is slow-cooked in sealed pots, every masala is freshly ground, and every dish carries the soul of the Nizam's kitchens. We are more than a restaurant — we are a journey.

Family-friendly, welcoming, and filled with the aromas of saffron, cardamom, and slow-cooked love. Come hungry. Leave happy.
